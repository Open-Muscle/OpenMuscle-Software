# Movement sheet

Print this page and keep it next to the computer. It goes with
[first-model.md](first-model.md). Tick each box when the card after the take says **Saved:** **Good**
(or **Check** and you are happy with it).

Participant code: ____________   Date: ____________   Bands on: left [ ]  right [ ]

## Every take

1. Press **Record**.
2. 0:00 to 0:05: hand completely still and relaxed.
3. 0:05 to about 0:30: the movement for this take, slow and smooth.
4. Press **Stop** at about 0:30. Never under 0:10.
5. Read the card. Press **Record next take**.

Keep the camera hand in view the whole time. Do not move the bands.

## Round one

| Done | Take | Name | Movement | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | 1 | `01-open-close` | Open the hand wide, close to a loose fist, repeat | 8 cycles of about 3 s |
| [ ] | 2 | `02-single-fingers` | One finger at a time, curl down and straighten, twice each: thumb, index, middle, ring, little (skip the thumb with a LASK5) | about 5 s per finger |
| [ ] | 3 | `03-grips` | Fist, pinch (thumb to index), point (index out, others curled), then once more through the three | each grip: close 1 s, hold 2 s, open 1 s, rest 1 s |
| [ ] | 4 | `04-free-mix` | Any mix of the above, with short pauses | free, until 0:30 |

## Round two

Same four movements, in the same order, at your natural pace.

| Done | Take | Name | Movement |

| [ ] | 8 | `08-free-mix` | as take 4 (this one is kept aside to test the model) |

In a hurry: skip takes 4 and 7 (six takes, about 3 minutes of recording).

## After the last take

1. Scroll to **Data & Models**. Tick the takes. Untick any **Bad** one.
2. Press **Train**. Wait.
3. Read the headline: **Good** is 75% or more, **Fair** is 50% or more, **Weak** is
   25% or more, **Not working yet** is below 25%.
4. Read **What to do next:** on the card and do it.
5. Press **Predict** if it is not already running. Watch the amber hand follow the
   green one.

## If a take looks wrong

| The card says | Do this |
|---|---|
| **hand lost** or **labels missing** | keep your hand in the camera's view; record the take again |
| **idle labeler** | move more; record the take again |
| **weak signal** | the band is not touching the arm; adjust it and begin again from take 1 |
| **too short** | record again, 30 seconds |
